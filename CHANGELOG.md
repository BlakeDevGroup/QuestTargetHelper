# Changelog

The newest section is uploaded to CurseForge as the release notes.

## 0.9.0
First beta for WoW Forever.
- Kill zone on nameplates (Blizzard and Plater) and target/focus frames, with a skull when your DoTs are lethal.
- Pull tracker: nearby quest mobs and mobs fighting your side, with live objective counts. Left-click to target, right-click to skull.
- Target next quest mob keybind with skull marking.
- Quest complete announcement, one-key looting setup, nameplate range setting.
- Open world only: switches off in dungeons and raids, where Forever hides nameplates and mob info from addons.
